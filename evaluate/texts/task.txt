Holds. `POST /books/:id/holds` places a hold on a book for the calling member; every member may place holds for themselves. It takes no body. It answers 201 with the hold as a JSON object that has an `id` field, 409 if the member already has an active hold on that book, and 404 for an unknown book. `POST /holds/:id/cancel` cancels a hold and takes no body. A member may cancel their own holds, and librarians and admins may cancel anyone's. It answers 200 with the hold, 403 when the caller may not cancel that hold, 409 if it is already cancelled, and 404 for an unknown hold.

Overdue fines. When `POST /loans/:id/return` comes one or more full days after the loan's `due_at`, it also charges the member a fine of 25 cents for each full day late, linked to the loan. `POST /fines/:id/waive` waives whatever is still owed on a fine and takes no body; librarians and admins may waive. It answers 200 with the fine in the shape `GET /members/:id/fines` uses, now with `balance_cents` 0, 409 if nothing is left to waive, and 404 for an unknown fine. A waived fine no longer counts toward what the member owes.

Nightly reconciliation. A job registered with the scheduler as `nightly-reconcile` writes one `reports` row of kind `nightly-reconcile` for the current UTC day each time it runs. Its body is a JSON object with `period` (YYYY-MM-DD) and these counts for that day: `borrows`, `returns`, `holds_placed`, `holds_cancelled`, `fines_charged`, `fines_charged_cents`, `fines_waived`, `fines_waived_cents`. `GET /reports/nightly/latest` returns the newest one in the shape `GET /reports/daily/latest` uses; librarians and admins may read it.

Hold queues. `GET /books/:id/holds` lists the book's active holds, oldest first, as a JSON array of holds; librarians and admins may read it, and it answers 404 for an unknown book. `GET /members/:id/holds` lists that member's active holds, oldest first. A member may read their own, and librarians and admins may read anyone's; it answers 403 for anyone else and 404 for an unknown member.

Renewal limits. `POST /loans/:id/renew` answers 409, and changes nothing, when the loan has already been renewed twice, or when a member other than the borrower has an active hold on the loan's book. Otherwise it behaves as it does now.

Member suspension. `POST /members/:id/suspend` and `POST /members/:id/unsuspend` take no body; librarians and admins may call them. Each answers 200 with the member in the shape `GET /members/:id` uses, which now includes `suspended`, true or false; 409 if the member is already in that state; and 404 for an unknown member. While a member is suspended, a loan for them and a hold they place are both refused with 409. Suspending a member changes none of their existing loans or holds.

Weekly summary. A job registered with the scheduler as `weekly-summary` writes one `reports` row of kind `weekly-summary` for the current UTC day each time it runs. Its body is a JSON object with `period_start` and `period_end` (YYYY-MM-DD), covering the seven UTC days that end with the current one, and the eight counts the nightly report has, summed over those days. `GET /reports/weekly/latest` returns the newest one in the shape `GET /reports/daily/latest` uses; librarians and admins may read it.

Member statements. `GET /members/:id/statement` returns a JSON object with the member's `balance_cents` and a `fines` array holding every fine the member has ever been charged, newest first, each in the shape `GET /members/:id/fines` uses, with `paid_cents` and `waived_cents`. A member may read their own statement, and librarians and admins may read anyone's; it answers 403 for anyone else and 404 for an unknown member.
